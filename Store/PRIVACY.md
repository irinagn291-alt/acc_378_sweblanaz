Dessert Book privacy policy · 15 November 2026

Dessert Book is free and has no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts operated by the developer or purchases. The recipe catalog and serving illustrations are included in the application. There is no recipe API or developer-operated backend.

Your favorites, notes, baking plans, progress, shopping list and timer are stored on your device. Timer and shopping data are shared with this app’s widgets through its own App Group. Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. Apple controls account storage and availability.

Notifications are local and optional. Reminder access is requested only when you send a planned bake to Apple Reminders. The app creates or updates only the reminders you export. Removing a plan does not delete an exported reminder. No camera, microphone, contacts, health or location access is used.

Delete the app to remove its local data. Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. Manage exported reminders in Apple Reminders. Use the app’s Preferences to read this policy summary offline. This document is ready for publication at the distributor’s privacy URL; no public URL or support identity is invented in this project.
