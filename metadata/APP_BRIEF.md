# Sweblanaz

## What it is

Sweblanaz is a free, offline dessert cookbook for home bakers. It contains 12 illustrated recipes and helps users find a bake, scale its ingredients, shop for it, schedule it, and follow it step by step.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A cold launch opens the “Recipes” tab directly under the title “Sweblanaz.” There is no welcome walkthrough, sign-in, or permission prompt on first launch.
2. The opening copy is “A LITTLE HOMEMADE HAPPINESS,” “Something sweet,  
   made by you.” and “Find your next favorite bake.”
3. “Recipe of the day” shows a recipe photo, category, time, name, and introduction. Tap the card or “Open the recipe” to open its details. Below it, “All recipes” is selected and “The recipe shelf” shows the full collection.
4. The tabs are “Recipes,” “Shopping list,” “Baking plan,” and “Favorites.” The sliders icon on “Recipes” opens “Preferences.” On a later launch, a saved timer can appear on “Recipes.”

All labels and copy below are quoted from the current English app unless another language is identified.

## Screens

### “Recipes” — “Sweblanaz”

- “Search recipes or ingredients” searches recipe names, introductions, and ingredient names. Every word entered must match somewhere in those fields.
- “All recipes,” “In season,” “Cakes,” “Everyday treats,” and “No bake” filter “The recipe shelf.” Cards show the recipe name, photo, category, and time in “min.” Tap a card to open its recipe.
- “Ingredients” opens a filter sheet titled “Ingredients.” It says “Show recipes that include every selected ingredient. Other ingredients may still be needed.” Tap an ingredient name to toggle its checkmark. “Reset” clears ingredient selections. “Done” closes the sheet and shows the filtered shelf.
- After an ingredient is selected, the filter button reads “Products selected.” “Clear filters” clears the search, category, and ingredient selections and restores “All recipes.”
- The footer reads “Always in your kitchen. All recipes work offline.” The sliders icon has the accessibility label “Preferences.”

### Recipe details — the recipe’s name

- Shows the illustration, recipe name, introduction, time, and category. The heart has the accessibility label “Save as favorite” or “Remove from favorites.” It toggles whether the recipe appears in “Favorites.”
- “Let’s bake” opens “Step by step.” After advancing through a recipe, the button reads “Continue cooking.”
- “Plan this recipe” opens the scheduling form with the recipe’s currently selected portions. “Oven timer” opens the timer with a default of 20 “min.”
- “Ingredients” shows quantities and a plus/minus stepper displaying the count with “portion” or “portions.” It supports 1–48 portions and remembers the selection for that recipe. When scaled, the screen says “Ingredient amounts are scaled. Round eggs sensibly; pan size and cooking time may need adjusting.”
- “Add to shopping list” adds the ingredient quantities for the selected portions. “Update shopping list” replaces the quantity for that recipe’s existing shopping entry. Both leave the recipe open. The “Allergens:” line is below the shopping button.
- “The method” shows the full numbered instructions. “Your kitchen notes” contains an editable field with the placeholder “What would you try next time?” Notes save as the user types. The image disclosure reads “Serving images are AI-created illustrations. Results vary with ingredients and equipment.”
- The system back control returns to the previous screen. A recipe opened from the daily-recipe widget has “Close” to dismiss it.

### “Step by step”

- Shows the recipe name, progress bar, “Step” with the current and total step numbers, and the current instruction. “Next step” advances. “Back” appears from the second step onward and moves one step back.
- Timed steps show “Start timer” followed by “ · ” and the suggested duration in “min.” The button opens “Oven timer” with that duration. An existing timer can also be controlled here while a step is displayed.
- “Finish recipe” on the final step shows “Made with a little love.” and “Your recipe is complete. Save a note about how it went.” “Start again” returns to the first step. “Done” returns to the recipe, where the notes field is available.
- “Close” returns to the recipe. The footer says “Your place is saved automatically, even when you close the app.”

### “Oven timer”

- Shows the recipe name and a plus/minus duration stepper from 1 to 360 “min.” “Notify me when ready” starts off. The explanation reads “If you enable this, iOS asks to send a local alert. The timer works without notification permission.”
- “Start timer” starts the countdown and closes the sheet. “Close” dismisses the sheet without starting a timer.
- If a timer already exists, the sheet shows its status, countdown, recipe name, and controls. The same timer card can appear on “Recipes,” “Baking plan,” and an active cooking step.
- The status reads “In the oven” while running, “Paused” while held, and “Your bake is ready to check” at zero. “Pause” holds the remaining time, “Resume” continues it, “Cancel timer” removes it, and “Done” clears the finished timer.
- The sheet says “A kitchen aid, not a safety alarm. Check your oven and food regularly. Only one timer can run at a time.”

### “Shopping list”

- Opening copy reads “A THOUGHTFUL LITTLE LIST,” “Ready for the pantry.” and “Shared ingredients, neatly added together.”
- Each ingredient row is labeled by its name and quantity. Tap it to check it off or uncheck it. Checked items are struck through, and the count ends with “checked off.” Ingredients measured in the same unit are combined across recipes.
- “On your menu” lists the recipes contributing ingredients. Each row has a portion stepper from 1 to 96 and a minus-circle button with the accessibility label “Remove” followed by the recipe name. Changing the portions recalculates quantities. Removing a recipe takes its quantities out of the list.
- “Share the list” opens the iOS share sheet with the ingredient names, quantities, and checkmarks as text.
- “Clear list” opens “Remove all recipes from the shopping list?” Confirm with “Clear list” or dismiss to keep the list.

### “Baking plan”

- Opening copy reads “MAKE ROOM FOR GOOD THINGS,” “A week worth baking.” and “Plan a few bakes, one lovely day at a time.” A saved timer appears above “Add a recipe.”
- “Add a recipe” opens “Choose a recipe.” Tap a recipe name to open “Plan this recipe,” or tap “Cancel” to return.
- Saved entries appear in date order with a photo, date and time, recipe name, and portion count. Tap the recipe name for its details. The trash icon, labeled “Remove” for accessibility, removes the plan entry.
- “Send to Reminders” requests access as needed and adds a reminder to the default Apple Reminders list with the recipe name, planned date and time, and an alarm. Its note reads “Planned in Sweblanaz. Open the app for ingredients and steps.” After an export, the button reads “Update reminder.” Success is reported as “Saved in Apple Reminders.”
- “Add next 7 days to shopping” adds planned portions from today through the following six calendar days to the shopping list. The app stays on the plan and displays “Planned portions were added to your shopping list.”
- The footer reads “Reminders are optional. Removing a plan here keeps any reminder you exported in Apple Reminders.”

### “Plan this recipe”

- Shows the recipe name, a “Date and time” picker, and a portion stepper from 1 to 48. The time starts about an hour ahead. The picker allows scheduling from the present through roughly one year ahead.
- The form says “The plan is saved on this device. You can optionally send an entry to Apple Reminders from the plan screen.”
- “Save to plan” adds a new entry and closes the form. “Cancel” closes it without saving. Saved entries have no edit form. To change an entry, remove it and add it again.

### “Favorites”

- Opening copy reads “KEEP THE GOOD ONES CLOSE,” “Your well-loved pages.” and “Recipes to come back to, again and again.”
- Shows the recipe cards saved with the heart. Tap a recipe to open its details. “Remove from favorites” on the recipe removes its card from this tab.

### “Preferences”

- “Export file” opens the system save picker and suggests “Sweblanaz - Recipe folio.” “Import file” opens the system file picker. A successful import shows “File imported.” Import merges notes, favorites, and plans, using the newer saved change when the same item exists in both copies. An incompatible file produces a system error message.
- “Local notifications” says “Choose a gentle weekly nudge or seasonal inspiration. Alerts are scheduled on your device, with no server.” “Sunday planning · 10:00” schedules a weekly local notification. “New season · first day at 10:00” schedules notifications for March 1, June 1, September 1, and December 1. “Open system settings” opens the app’s settings in iOS.
- The weekly notification reads “A little baking plan” and “Choose something lovely to bake for this week.” The seasonal notification reads “A new season in your kitchen” and “Explore the seasonal collection in Sweblanaz.” A timer notification uses “Your bake is ready to check” with the recipe name.
- “Privacy” says “No ads, purchases, analytics or tracking. Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. Shopping and timers are shared only with your widgets. Reminder export uses Apple Reminders only when requested. No camera, microphone or location access.” It repeats “Always in your kitchen. All recipes work offline.”
- “About” shows “Version” and “1.3.2 (17).” Its copy reads “A small, free cookbook for everyday home baking. Metric measures. A teaspoon is 5 ml; a tablespoon is 15 ml. Seasons follow the Northern Hemisphere calendar. Adjust cooking times to your oven.”
- “Done” closes “Preferences.”

### Home Screen and Lock Screen

- The widget is “Your kitchen page.” Its description is “A daily recipe, your oven timer and the next bakes. Always offline.” The small and medium Home Screen widgets show “RECIPE OF THE DAY” and the recipe name. The medium size also shows timer status. Tapping opens the daily recipe, or “Recipes” when the medium widget has a timer.
- The large widget shows “This week’s baking,” up to four upcoming entries within the seven-day window, timer status, and up to four ingredients under “Shopping list.” When empty it says “Choose your next recipes in the baking plan.” and “Add recipe ingredients in the app.” Tapping it opens “Baking plan.”
- Lock Screen widgets show timer status. Depending on size and state, text includes “In the oven,” “Paused,” “Your bake is ready to check,” “No active oven timer,” or “Oven.” Tapping opens “Recipes.” A timer can also appear as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on supported devices with Live Activities enabled. Tapping it opens “Recipes” for the timer controls.

## Features

- “Recipe of the day” and “The recipe shelf,” with 12 illustrated recipes available offline.
- “Search recipes or ingredients,” category and seasonal browsing, and an “Ingredients” filter that requires every selected ingredient.
- Adjustable portions, scaled ingredients, “Allergens:” information, and the full instructions under “The method.”
- “Step by step” cooking with saved progress, suggested durations for timed steps, and a completion screen.
- “Oven timer” with pause, resume, cancel, an optional local notification, widgets, and a Live Activity on supported devices.
- “Shopping list” with combined quantities, ingredient checkmarks, per-recipe portion adjustment, removal, sharing, and clearing.
- “Baking plan” with date, time, and portions, optional Apple Reminders export, and seven-day shopping-list additions.
- “Favorites,” private “Your kitchen notes,” and manual transfer of favorites, notes, and plans through “Export file” and “Import file.”
- Optional “Sunday planning · 10:00” and “New season · first day at 10:00” local notifications.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- **A search or filter hides the daily card.** “Recipe of the day” returns with an empty search, “All recipes,” and zero selected ingredients. If the shelf says “No recipe found” and “Try fewer ingredients or a different search.”, loosen the search, switch to “All recipes,” or use “Clear filters” when ingredients are selected. “Reset” in the “Ingredients” sheet clears that sheet’s selections only.
- **Empty tabs need input.** “Your basket is waiting” and “Open a recipe and add its ingredients here.” are cleared by using “Add to shopping list” on a recipe. “A fresh page for your week” and “Choose a recipe and a time to make it.” are cleared by saving a plan. “A place for your favorites” and “Tap the heart on a recipe to keep it here.” are cleared by using “Save as favorite.”
- **Shopping changes reset checkmarks.** Adding, updating, removing, or changing a recipe’s portions clears all checked items. “Add next 7 days to shopping” adds to the current quantities every time it is tapped, including when the same plans were already added. It also clears checkmarks and can report success when the plan has no dates in the seven-day window. Open “Shopping list” to check the quantities and adjust or remove the affected recipes.
- **Closing cooking keeps the current step.** “Close” exits the guide. At step one the recipe still says “Let’s bake.” After advancing it says “Continue cooking,” and a completed recipe reopens at the completion screen. Use “Start again” there to reset it.
- **A finished timer still occupies the timer slot.** “Oven timer” shows the existing timer until it is cleared. Use “Cancel timer” while running or paused, or “Done” when it shows “Your bake is ready to check.” The app can also display “Finish or cancel your current timer first.”
- **Notification settings depend on iOS permission.** With notifications denied, the weekly and seasonal switches do not turn on, and a requested timer still starts. The notice reads “Notifications are off. The timer and planner still work. You can enable alerts in system settings.” Use “Open system settings” to enable them and try the switch again. Other possible notices are “The alert could not be scheduled. Your timer and plan are saved.” and “The timer is running. Live Activity is unavailable on this device.” The countdown remains available in the app.
- **Saving a plan and exporting a reminder are separate actions.** “Save to plan” closes the form. Use “Send to Reminders” on the saved entry to export it. If access is declined, the notice reads “Reminders access was declined. Your baking plan is still saved here.” Enable access in iOS settings and retry. If it says “No writable Reminders list is available. Create one in Apple Reminders and try again.”, create the list there and retry. A failed export can say “The reminder could not be saved. Your plan is safe in Sweblanaz.” Removing a plan requires a separate deletion in Apple Reminders if the exported reminder should also be removed.
- App notices appear under “Sweblanaz” and dismiss with “OK.” If local saving fails, the message is “Could not save changes. Free some storage and try again.”

## Permissions

- **Notifications.** iOS asks the first time the user starts a timer with “Notify me when ready” enabled or turns on “Sunday planning · 10:00” or “New season · first day at 10:00.” The system supplies the prompt copy. The app has no custom notification usage-description string.
- **Apple Reminders full access.** Requested when the user taps “Send to Reminders” or “Update reminder.” The current English usage description is “Sweblanaz can add a planned bake to Apple Reminders when you choose to export it.” The same description is used for both Reminders usage descriptions in each supported language.
  - German — “Sweblanaz kann einen Backtermin auf deinen Wunsch in Apple Erinnerungen speichern.”
  - French — “Sweblanaz peut ajouter une préparation à Rappels lorsque vous choisissez de l’exporter.”
  - Brazilian Portuguese — “Sweblanaz pode adicionar um preparo ao app Lembretes quando você escolher exportá-lo.”

## Absent

Login and accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, account deletion, and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are absent. Users can create private notes and plans and share copies through iOS. There is no public posting, recipe submission, or in-app community.

## Data and support

Recipes and saved changes are available offline. Data stays on the device by default, with local access for the app’s widgets. There is no automatic account or device sync. “Export file” transfers favorites, notes, and plans to a user-chosen destination. “Import file” merges that data into the current device. Shopping, timer state, cooking progress, and portion preferences are outside that transfer. “Share the list” shares a shopping-list copy, and “Send to Reminders” writes a reminder only when requested. There is no on-screen contact or support control.

## Scanning and health

There is no barcode or QR scanning. Recipes show food-allergen and ingredient-safety information under “Allergens:”. “Berry yogurt cups” says “Milk, oats. Honey is unsuitable for babies under 12 months.” “Honey oat cookies” says “Wheat, oats, milk. No honey for babies under 12 months.” “Fudgy cocoa brownies” says “Wheat, eggs, milk. Check chocolate label.” There are no on-screen citations, nutrition panels, or medical or product-health lookup screens.

## Platform

- iPhone and iPad, with a minimum of iOS 17.4.
- iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
- Native text and recipes are available in English, German, French, and Brazilian Portuguese. English is the fallback. The localized transfer controls and success message are “Export file,” “Import file,” and “File imported” in English, “Datei exportieren,” “Datei importieren,” and “Datei importiert” in German, “Exporter un fichier,” “Importer un fichier,” and “Fichier importé” in French, and “Exportar arquivo,” “Importar arquivo,” and “Arquivo importado” in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Dates, times, and quantity formatting follow the device’s locale and calendar. Recipes use metric measures and Celsius across regions. Seasonal browsing follows the Northern Hemisphere calendar. The native flows have no region gate and require no location access.
